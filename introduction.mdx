Hi, I'm MX Quan. I spend a lot of time thinking — about how we learn, how ideas connect, and why certain ways of seeing the world seem to unlock so much more than others. Writing is how I make sense of that thinking: I treat essays less as finished arguments and more as live documents of a mind working things out. Beyond writing, I read broadly across disciplines, and I build small utility tools as a hobby — not because I set out to be a developer, but because building something that solves a real problem is one of the most satisfying feelings I know.

My philosophy of writing is straightforward: I share raw thinking, not polished conclusions. You won't find confident ten-step frameworks here. What you will find are honest attempts to understand difficult things — questions I'm still sitting with, ideas I'm stress-testing, and occasional moments where something clicks. I'd rather show the process than pretend the answer was always obvious.

<Note>
  This blog is a living space. Essays get revised, ideas evolve, and older posts sometimes get updated with new thinking. If something feels unfinished, that's intentional — it means I'm still working on it.
</Note>
